We can expect a really large building to depress the Earth's surface somewhat, but this can be reduced by spreading-out the base of the structure. Some of such a thing has been suggested already here in the Half-Bakery, under "Pyramid Skyscrapers". It may be impossible to construct a really really big building without it looking something like a wide cone or pyramid. Concrete is actually an impressive substance with enormous vertical-load-bearing "compression strength" and approximately the same density as, of all things (!), aluminum.
